About the song:

"Haldern," off Black Country, New Road's critically acclaimed 2022 album Ants From Up There, is a sweeping chamber-post-rock track born 
directly from a spontaneous jam at Germany's Haldern Pop Festival. Driven by a hypnotic, looping piano line, lush string flourishes, 
and dynamic saxophones, the song gradually transforms from an intimate, uneasy groove into a chaotic, cathartic wall of sound. Lyrically, 
former frontman Isaac Wood delivers a raw, deeply vulnerable performance about emotional codependency and desperation, using stark imagery 
of physical boundaries and overwhelming devotion to capture the claustrophobic weight of trying to hold on to a fading connection. Through 
tracks like "Haldern", "Concorde", "The Place Where He Inserted the Blade", and numerous others, BCNR helped reshape the modern indie scene 
by spearheading a wave of complex, experimental music that rejected traditional verse-chorus formulas, in the process cultivating an 
extraordinarily dedicated cult following that turned them into one of the most celebrated and fiercely debated underground bands of their 
generation.
